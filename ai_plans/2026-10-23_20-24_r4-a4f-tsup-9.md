## Problem

tsup 9.0.0 (GitHub release notes) drops Node below 22.18 (we run 22.23.3) and moves its debug logging to obug;
it also upgrades its esbuild and chokidar and stops passing `baseUrl` from tsconfig. Neither of our two configs
(`packages/types/tsup.config.ts`, `apps/cli/tsup.config.ts`) sets an option that changed.

## Change

The catalog bump only.
