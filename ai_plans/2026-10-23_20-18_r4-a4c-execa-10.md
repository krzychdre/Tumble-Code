## Problem

execa 10 (release notes of v10.0.0) requires Node 22 (we run 22.23), makes the subprocess a plain promise with
the Node `ChildProcess` moved to `subprocess.nodeChildProcess`, removes `execaCommand`, the `'ipc'` stdio entry,
and lets `input` win over an inherited stdin. Grep of every execa call site (`ExecaTerminalProcess.ts`,
`packages/core/src/custom-tools/esbuild-runner.ts`, `apps/cli/src/commands/cli/doctor.ts`, the CLI integration
scripts): none uses `.on()`, `.send()`, `.ref()`, `execaCommand`, `ipc` or `input`. `ExecaTerminalProcess` uses
`.pid`, `.kill()` and `.iterable()`, which stay on the execa subprocess.

The only fallout is a type: the field was `ReturnType<typeof execa>`, whose `iterable()` chunks are `unknown` in
10.x, so the wrapper that decoded `Uint8Array` chunks no longer type-checks. That wrapper never ran: without
`encoding` or `binary` options execa yields strings.

## Change

- Catalog bump.
- `subprocess` is typed `ResultPromise<{ all: true; stdin: "ignore" }>`, so `iterable({ from: "all" })` is an
  `AsyncIterable<string>` and goes to `setActiveStream` directly; the decode wrapper is gone.
