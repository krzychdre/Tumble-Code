## Problem

chokidar 6.0.0 (GitHub release notes) rewrites the watcher, renames `interval`/`binaryInterval` to
`pollingInterval`/`pollingBinaryInterval` (old names stay as deprecated aliases), makes `close()` terminal
(`add()` throws afterwards), validates options in the constructor, and declares `engines.node >= 22.22`.

Our only use is `McpConnectionManager.setupFileWatcher`: two `chokidar.watch(path, {})` calls with no options,
a `change` listener each, and `close()` when the server's watchers are replaced or disposed. A replaced watcher
is always a new `chokidar.watch`, never `add()` on a closed one. Nothing we pass is renamed or validated
differently.

The Node floor: the extension host runs on VS Code's Electron Node, and `engines.vscode` is `^1.102.0`, whose
Node is older than 22.22. The package source (`index.js`, `backend.js`, `runtime.js`, `tree.js`, `reconcile.js`,
`policy.js` and `readdirp` 5.1.1) uses only `fs.watch`, `fs/promises` `lstat`/`stat`/`readdir`/`realpath`/`open`/
`readlink`, `path`, `os.type` and `events`, all present in every Node 22. The 22.22 floor is a support policy,
not an API need.
