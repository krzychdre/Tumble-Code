## Problem

nock 15 (`migration_guides/migrating_to_15.md`) moves events and `reply` callbacks to `Request` objects, removes
`delayBody`/`delayConnection`, matches the `Host` header, and answers an unmatched request with a 501 response
instead of throwing. It also exports only the default `nock` object: `import { back } from "nock"` no longer
type-checks.

Our use: `src/vitest.setup.ts` calls `nock.disableNetConnect()` for every `src` spec, four wire specs open
`127.0.0.1` with `enableNetConnect` or `restore`/`activate`, and `openrouter.spec.ts` replays fixtures through
`nock.back`. None uses a `reply` callback, the events, the delay methods or `reqheaders`.

## Change

- Bump to ^15.0.5.
- `openrouter.spec.ts` reads `nock.back` from the default export.
