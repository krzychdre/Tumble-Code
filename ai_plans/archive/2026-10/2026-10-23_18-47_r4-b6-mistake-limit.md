**Status:** done.

## Problem

"A limit of 0 means the default for background tasks" was written in the Task constructor (for the tool repetition
detector) and again in `TaskApiLoop.handleBackgroundMistakeLimit`. The constructor also applied
`?? DEFAULT_CONSECUTIVE_MISTAKE_LIMIT` to a parameter whose default is already that constant.

## Change

- `Task.effectiveMistakeLimit` (readonly) is computed once in the constructor: the configured limit, except that a
  background task with a limit of 0 gets `DEFAULT_CONSECUTIVE_MISTAKE_LIMIT`. The repetition detector and
  `handleBackgroundMistakeLimit` (through `TaskApiLoopAccess.effectiveMistakeLimit`) read it.
- The redundant `??` default is gone. Foreground tasks keep reading `consecutiveMistakeLimit` (0 still means no limit).

## Tests

- `Task.spec.ts`: one parametrized case pins `effectiveMistakeLimit` for a foreground limit of 0 (stays 0), a
  background limit of 0 (the default, 3) and a background limit of 5.
- `TaskApiLoop.background-mistake-limit.spec.ts` feeds `effectiveMistakeLimit`; its "limit of 0" case moved to the
  Task case above, where the rule now lives.
- Mutation checks: dropping the background rule fails the background-0 case; dropping `|| !isBackground` fails the
  foreground-0 case.
