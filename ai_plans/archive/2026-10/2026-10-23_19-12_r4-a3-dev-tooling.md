## Not in this branch

`prettier` stays on 3.5.3. Version 3.9.10 formats about 70 TypeScript files and the workflow files differently
from 3.5.3. The pre-commit hook formats every staged file, so the bump would scatter unrelated reformatting into
the next PRs of every lane. It needs its own branch: bump, `prettier --write` on the whole tree in one commit,
merged while no other branch is open.
