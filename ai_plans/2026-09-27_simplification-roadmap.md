# Simplification roadmap (2026-09-27)

Goal: code a person can understand and change with a text editor and `grep`, that is fast, and that survives
interruptions (cancel, crash, network loss, a corrupted file). Guiding rules: DRY (one place per fact), YAGNI
(delete what has one user or none), open/closed (add a tool, provider or setting by adding, not by editing ten
files).

Every item below was found by reading the code at `e9d27d1`. Items marked "verified" were re-checked by hand.
Effort: S under a day, M a few days, L a week or more.
