The CLI spinner has a new word list, themed on a rock tumbler: the barrel machine that turns sharp gravel into smooth stones by rolling it for days with water and grit. Twenty words for the machine running (Rumbling, Clattering, Thrumming, Kerplunking) and twenty for the lapidary work itself (Grinding, Lapping, Honing, Burnishing, Faceting, Sluicing). The consultancy vocabulary is gone: no more Combobulating, Actualizing, Interpolating or Reconciling, and no more kitchen metaphors.
