"tumble-code": minor
---

A "Tumble Code" entry at the right end of the status bar opens Tumble Code in a new editor tab, also when another Tumble tab is already open (the new tab opens next to the Tumble tab you used last). The Tumble button in the editor title bar still brings the open tab to the front; unlike that button, the status bar entry is always there, also when no file is open. To hide it, right-click the status bar and untick "Tumble Code".
