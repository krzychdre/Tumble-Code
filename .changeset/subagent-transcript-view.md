A subagent's whole transcript can now be opened from the subagents panel or from its row in the task history: its subtask, its reasoning, every tool call with its diff, every command with its output, and its final answer, shown like a normal chat and updated live while it works. The task you are in stays open and keeps running. A running subagent can be stopped, answered and guided from there. Panel rows now say what each running subagent is doing (for example `$ npm test`) and why a failed one stopped.
