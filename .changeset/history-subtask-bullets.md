Subtasks in the task history are shown as a list marked with dashes. A working subtask shows its spinner in place of the dash, so its text lines up with the rest of the list.

Each subtask in the task history shows a details line under its title: its mode, start time, whether it finished, its cost and its tokens in and out.

The task history shows the cost of a task together with all of its subtasks, the same total the cloud reports. Before, it showed only the parent task's own cost. Hover the cost to see the task's own part. Sorting by most expensive uses the same total.
