Subtasks in the task history are shown as a list marked with dashes. A working subtask shows its spinner in place of the dash, so its text lines up with the rest of the list.

Every task and subtask in the task history shows the same summary line: its mode, start time (the full date for an older task), whether it finished with a result, its cost and its tokens in and out.

The task history shows the cost and tokens of a task together with all of its subtasks, the same total the cloud reports. Before, it showed only the parent task's own cost. Hover the cost or the tokens to see the task's own part. Sorting by most expensive uses the same total.
