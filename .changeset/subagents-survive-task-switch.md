Subagents that are still working stay in the subagents panel when you leave their task and come back. Before, reopening the task showed an empty panel, then only the subagents started after you came back. The finished run's saved list also missed the others.

The subagents panel header counts running subagents separately from subagents waiting in the queue and subagents waiting for your answer. Before, a run with 4 subagents working and 16 queued read "20/20 active".
