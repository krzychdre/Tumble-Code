Subtasks work with tasks running in the background. A task you left that starts a subtask hands over in the background: the subtask starts there in its own mode and model, without changing the mode or model shown in the panel. When a subtask that runs in the background finishes, its parent continues in the background, or on screen if you are looking at it. Opening a parent whose subtask is still working shows its conversation without the "Resume" button.
