Claude models on Anthropic, Vertex and Bedrock now have a "Model Reasoning Effort" setting next to the thinking toggle. It sends the chosen level (low, medium, high, xhigh or max, as each model offers them) and also works with thinking off, which matters for Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Fable 5.x, where thinking cannot be turned off. Left unset, the model's own default applies.
