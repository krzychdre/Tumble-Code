Claude models on Vertex and Bedrock now offer their real maximum output (for example 64K for Haiku 4.5 and Sonnet 4.5, 128K for Sonnet 4.6) instead of 8192. Claude 4.6 and later models use their native 1M context window at standard prices on Anthropic, Vertex and Bedrock, and Sonnet 4 and 4.5 with the 1M beta on Bedrock are now priced at the long-context rates. The retired Claude Sonnet 4 and Opus 4 (May 2025) are no longer offered for the Anthropic provider.
