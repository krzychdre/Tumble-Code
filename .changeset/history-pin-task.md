You can now pin a task with the pin button on its row in the task history and in the recent tasks on the welcome screen. Pinned tasks stay at the top under a "Pinned" header, the most recently pinned first, and no longer drift down as new tasks arrive; a pinned task that is running keeps its place and still shows that it works. The pin survives reloads; the same button unpins the task.
